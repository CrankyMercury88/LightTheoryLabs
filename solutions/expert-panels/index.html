Calibrated expert panels for creative AI training

A managed, calibrated panel of senior editors, colorists, sound designers, motion designers and voice talent. The panel writes tasks, cuts reference answers and grades agent output for our own environments, and can be engaged directly for your programs. The product is calibration and seniority, not headcount.

How it works
  1. 01ScopeCrafts, headcount, tools, weekly volume and the agreement threshold you need, agreed in writing.

Calibration

How graders come to agree.

Each panelist scores the same reference set blind. We measure agreement, discuss the disagreements, tighten the rubric and score again. The threshold is agreed with you and reported throughout the engagement.

  1. 01Score blindEvery panelist scores the same reference set without seeing the others.
  2. 02MeasureAgreement per dimension, with the widest disagreements pulled out.
  3. 03DiscussThe lead runs a session on each disagreement, with the clip on screen.
  4. 04TightenThe rubric gains an anchor or example, and the set is scored again.
  5. ↺Repeat until agreement clears the threshold
