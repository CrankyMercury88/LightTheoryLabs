Solutions · 02

Professional evaluation and post-training data for AI-generated video

Side-by-side preferences, rubric scores and written critiques of AI-generated video, produced by working editors, colorists and sound designers rather than a general rater pool. Scores cover cinematography, colour, sound and edit, and inter-rater agreement is published alongside the results. The same judgments serve two uses: measuring a model against professional taste, and training it through reward models and preference tuning. An Evaluation Pack of 500 judgments ships in two weeks.

How it works
  1. 01Fix the rubricWe adapt our four-dimension rubric to your model, prompts and failure modes, and freeze it.
  2. 02CalibrateGraders score a shared set blind until agreement clears the required threshold.
  3. 03Judge blindPairwise preferences and rubric scores with model identity hidden.
  4. 04CritiqueEvery low score carries a written reason cited to timecode.
  5. 05ReportScores, preferences, critiques and agreement, as a table and a JSONL export.
Two uses

Measure the model, then train it.

EvaluationBenchmark a model or checkpoint against professional judgment, with agreement data so the scores hold up.
Post-training dataPreferences and scored critiques at volume, ready for reward models, RLHF and DPO.

Questions
How is this different from crowd preference data?

The raters are working professionals, calibrated against each other before scoring, and we publish the agreement data. The signal is narrower and more expensive per judgment, and considerably more informative about craft.

Can we supply our own rubric?

Yes. We calibrate the panel on your criteria, or merge them with ours and report both.

Can this data be used for post-training?

Yes. Pairwise preferences and rubric scores ship in a format ready for reward modelling and preference optimisation, and critiques can be used as written feedback.

What formats do results ship in?

An HTML table in the report and a JSONL export with per-judgment scores, preferences, critiques and rater IDs.
