The problemFull analysis →

Agents can operate the software. They cannot yet judge the cut.

On Timeline-Bench, 56 real editing tasks that run from raw footage to a finished deliverable, the best of 16 frontier agents resolved 26.8% of tasks and the average agent resolved 14.0%. Of the runs that failed, 73% passed every delivery and content test and failed only the quality test.

The agents read footage as still frames and transcripts, inspected their renders for defects rather than pacing or story, and reported success in 93% of runs. Correctness can be verified by a script. Craft has to be taught by the people who have it, and that is what we supply.

Anatomy of an RL task

An environment is only as useful as its reward signal. Ours combines checks a script can verify with a craft rubric scored blind by a calibrated panel, and every grader is attacked by an engineer before it ships.

01BriefWritten the way a client writes it: audience, runtime, tone, delivery spec.
02Source footageOwned or licensed for AI training. Never client material.
03Starting stateA DaVinci Resolve project or open timeline file that restores identically on every attempt.
04Reference cutMade by a senior editor, with a narrated account of the decisions.
05GraderAutomatic checks for quotes, runtime, loudness and spec. A rubric for structure, pace and continuity.
06ScoreA verdict with reasons, cited to timecode, so the signal can be inspected and not just consumed.

Questions6
What is an RL environment for video editing?

A reproducible task an agent can attempt repeatedly: a brief, source footage, a starting project and a grader that scores the result, shipped with a senior editor's reference cut. The agent learns from the reward; the reference cut shows what the reward is pointing at.

Why are editing tasks hard for agents that already write code?

Most of what makes a cut good is not checkable by a script. On Timeline-Bench, 73% of failed runs passed every delivery and content test and failed only the human-calibrated quality test. The missing capability is judgment, which is why our graders include a calibrated panel.

Which tools do you build on?

DaVinci Resolve, open timeline formats (OTIO, EDL, FCPXML) and open-source tools such as Shotcut, Ardour and Blender. We do not build on tools whose licenses prohibit AI-training use.

How do your graders resist reward hacking?

Each grader pairs automatic checks with a blind craft rubric, and an engineer attempts to pass it without doing the work before it ships. Tasks that top agents can pass more than 30% of the time with five attempts do not enter the set.

How does an engagement start?

With a 30-minute call or live demo. We walk through a sample task, its grader and a baseline run, then send a written proposal within 48 hours. Scope depends on task volume, customisation and exclusivity, and is quoted in the proposal.

Can you work under a data vendor's contract?

Yes. We subcontract white-label or co-deliver, with NDA and non-circumvention signed first. We do not approach a vendor's client about work that vendor introduced.
